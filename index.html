About the Archive

The Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Library serves SJSU students and the San Jose community. This archive preserves events that influenced safety, public discussion, student activism, and the use of the building.

Event Timeline

2016: Fatal Fall at the Library

A person died after falling from an upper floor of the library. SJSU closed the building and offered counseling support. The event preceded later library safety renovations.

  • Read the SJSU announcement
  • Read the KTVU report

2017: Library Safety Renovations

The library changed parts of the building after deaths by suicide. The event raised questions about mental health support and public-space design.

Read the Mercury News report

2019: Gunfire Strikes the Library

Shots struck the library and a nearby parking structure. No injuries were reported, but students described the incident as frightening.

  • Read the ABC7 report
  • Read the KTVU report

2022: Replica Gun Incident

A woman with a replica gun barricaded herself inside a library bathroom. Police arrested her after she surrendered.

Read the KTVU report

2024: Bathroom Fires

Authorities investigated fires set in two library bathrooms. No injuries were reported.

  • ABC7 report
  • CBS News report

2024 to 2025: Controversial Day of the Dead Display

A Day of the Dead altar honoring Palestinian children led to complaints from SJSU faculty and a university review. The university determined that the display did not violate policy, raising questions about free expression and campus conflict.

Read the Jweekly report

2025: Public Services Budget Protest

City workers and librarians rallied outside the library against proposed budget cuts affecting San Jose public services.

Read the IFPTE report

2026: Student Protest at the AI Center

Students held a demonstration outside the library and placed a protest banner over the AI Center's sign.

Read the Fight Back! News report

2026: Altercation Leaves Two Injured

An altercation near and inside the library left two people injured. Police arrested one person.

Read the Spartan Daily report
